About

Senior Graphic Designer & Educator

I’m Elif Handegül Yılmaz, a senior graphic designer and educator with over 13 years of experience working with agencies and brands.

From brand identity and editorial design to events, social media, web interfaces, video and animation, I create cohesive visual communication solutions shaped around each brand’s needs.

My design process brings aesthetics, function and consistency together. Since 2022, I have also shared my knowledge and experience with emerging designers as a graphic design educator.
