Grand Seyahat — Poster Design

Concept and visual languagePoster and layout designTypography and color systemMockup presentation

Communication brief

The Grand Seyahat poster is designed to present travel planning, reservations and guidance services clearly on a single promotional surface. The design focuses on making the message quickly understandable in busy public settings such as an airport. Its main communication combines a travel-focused headline with service details and reservation information in one composition.

Composition and hierarchy

A purple and green palette creates contrast, while a yellow accent draws attention to the campaign headline. Circular photographs and a dotted route line reinforce the travel theme. The headline, service list and contact details occupy distinct areas, creating a clear reading order. Photography and typography work together, with visual movement balanced against the information that a viewer needs to find.

Presented work

Showing the poster at environmental scale allows the campaign headline and reservation message to be reviewed together. The complete display surface remains visible, making its boundaries, visual density and relationship with the surroundings clear. The result is a promotional design example that brings photography and typography around one message.
