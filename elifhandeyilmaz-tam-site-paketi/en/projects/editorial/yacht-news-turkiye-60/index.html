Yacht News Türkiye — Issue 60

Editorial designPage layoutVisual hierarchy

Publication and content

Yacht News Türkiye Issue 60 is a 72-page publication for July–August 2026. Its coverage includes the global superyacht industry, yacht design, festivals and summits. The editorial approach brings these subjects into one publication identity, balancing imagery and text while maintaining a recognizable visual language across the issue.

Design approach

The documented design approach emphasizes strong imagery and balanced page layouts. Headlines, body text and photographs work within a shared hierarchy so that different types of content can sit together coherently. The cover introduces the issue, while the interior pages carry its reading flow. This project page separates the cover preview from the complete PDF so visitors can inspect both the overall impression and the detailed layout.

Reviewable output

The cover below comes from the existing archive, and the full publication is available through the PDF link. Keeping the PDF separate means the larger publication file is loaded when a visitor chooses to review it. Page sequences, image placement and information hierarchy can be evaluated directly in the publication. The existing magazine gallery and flipbook on the homepage remain available independently of this static project page.
