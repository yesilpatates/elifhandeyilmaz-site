PilavYeri — Brand Identity

Logo designBrand visual languageIdentity applicationsProject presentation

Project and brief

PilavYeri is a restaurant brand combining traditional rice dishes with modern presentation and quick service. The identity project focuses on expressing that approach consistently through the logo and its applications. The presentation brings together the restaurant’s products, service setting and digital presence. It shows an identity system across several touchpoints rather than presenting the logo in isolation.

Visual approach

The logo combines a spoon symbol with flowing lettering. Deep green, warm light tones and turquoise connect the food imagery, palette cards and application examples. Light and dark backgrounds show how the identity appears on different surfaces. Presenting the logo, symbol and colors together makes the visual decisions easier to understand and demonstrates the relationship between the individual elements.

Presentation scope

The presentation includes mockups for business cards, bags, menus, textiles, signage and digital screens. These examples allow the identity to be reviewed across different scales and surfaces. The documented scope covers logo design, visual language, identity applications and presentation design. The presentation shows the relationship between small items such as business cards and larger applications such as signage. Grouping the palette with the application examples creates an overview of the identity and its character.
