Alfa Sigorta — Health Insurance Animation

8-bit visual languagePixel animationVertical social video

Project and storytelling

The Alfa Sigorta health insurance animation approaches product communication through a vertical social media video. The work uses an 8-bit game aesthetic and pixel animation. Presenting insurance communication in a distinctive visual language, it belongs to the portfolio’s video and motion category. This page gives the project a directly accessible address for review.

Visual language and format

Pixel-based graphics and a game-inspired aesthetic define the visual language of the work. The vertical format shapes its presentation in a social feed. The visual story can be reviewed through the sequence, movement and timing of its graphic elements. The still cover introduces its overall character, while the video link provides access to the complete animated piece. Together they allow both the static and moving expressions of the design to be reviewed.

Reviewing the video

The cover and viewing link refer to the same video, which can be watched in its vertical social media format. The cover introduces the visual aesthetic, while the video shows how the animated elements relate over time. The project is a selected example of insurance communication explored through a visual approach influenced by game culture.
